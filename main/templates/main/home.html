ABOUT SAVE LIFE

Connecting People When Every Second Matters

Save Life is a blood donation and emergency support platform designed to help people find blood donors when they need blood urgently.

During accidents, surgeries, medical treatments and other emergencies, finding the right blood group quickly can be difficult. Save Life aims to make this process easier by connecting donors and people in need.

Users can create an account, maintain their personal information, register as blood donors, search for blood and submit emergency requests.

DONOR AWARENESS

Who Can Donate Blood?

✓ Generally healthy individuals
✓ People meeting the required age criteria
✓ People who meet the required health criteria
✓ People who pass the blood donation screening

Important

Blood donation eligibility can depend on age, health, medicines, medical history and local blood-bank rules. Always follow the screening advice of a qualified healthcare professional or blood bank.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I donate blood?

Blood donation helps maintain blood supplies and can support patients who need blood during emergencies, surgeries and medical treatments.

How does Save Life help?

Save Life connects registered users and donors so that people can search for blood and submit emergency requests more easily.

Can I register as a donor?

Yes. After creating an account and logging in, you will be able to complete your donor registration.

Can I request blood during an emergency?

Yes. Logged-in users will be able to submit emergency blood requests through their dashboard.
